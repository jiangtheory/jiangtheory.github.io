Keeping polymers in use requires knowing what a material contains and how it will behave when processed again. Plastic waste often includes mixtures with different compositions and processing histories, making recovery and reuse difficult.

We study the relationship between composition, processing history, and material properties. This connects our work on plastic characterization with questions about which mixtures can be recovered or reprocessed, and where material quality is lost.

A recovered material must also be workable. It needs suitable flow behavior during processing and adequate properties after manufacture. Understanding these requirements helps connect material recovery with practical reuse and recycling decisions.
