We study how molecular structure and processing shape the behavior of polymers and other soft materials. Our work combines molecular simulation with physics-informed machine learning to connect material design with the demands of production and use.

</a>
<a class="research-theme" href="{{ '/research/circular-economy/' | relative_url }}">
<strong>Soft materials for the circular economy</strong>
<span>Understanding polymer mixtures and processing to support material recovery and reuse.</span>
</a>
<a class="research-theme" href="{{ '/research/physics-informed-ml/' | relative_url }}">
<strong>Physics-informed machine learning</strong>
<span>Using physical knowledge to predict material properties across molecular structures and conditions.</span>
