Polymer electrolytes and membranes must transport ions while maintaining mechanical strength and stability. Their performance depends on molecular motion, interactions, and organization.

We study how these molecular features shape transport and material response. Processing belongs in the same picture: mixing, flow, and temperature changes can alter the structure that forms and how it behaves in use.

Our goal is to connect those relationships with the demands of energy storage and use, helping guide the design of materials that perform reliably and can be manufactured consistently.
