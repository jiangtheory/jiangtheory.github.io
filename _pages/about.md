# Materials & Product Design Group (Jiang Group)

**Designing soft materials into real-world products.**  
We create **computationally guided design workflows** to discover and optimize **soft materials systems**—polymers, electrolytes, colloids, gels, and complex fluids—bridging molecular insight to manufacturable performance.

Our group integrates:
- **Molecular simulation** to resolve structure and transport at the nanoscale  
- **Data science** to learn interpretable structure–property–process relationships  
- **Process systems engineering & optimization** to navigate tradeoffs and design under constraints  

We are especially interested in soft materials for:
- **Energy & sustainability** (electrolytes, membranes, separations, circular materials)
- **Health applications** (soft interfaces, delivery and barrier materials, responsive gels)

### What we care about
We aim to answer questions like:
- *How do molecular interactions shape macroscopic performance and lifetime?*
- *Which design knobs matter most—and how do we quantify uncertainty and risk early?*
- *How can we turn materials discovery into a product design problem with constraints (cost, safety, scalability)?*

### How we work (research pillars)
**1) Molecular-to-product modeling**  
Link molecular simulation and coarse-grained models to product-level metrics (transport, mechanical response, stability).

**2) Data-efficient discovery**  
Build datasets, descriptors, and surrogate models that are *interpretable* and *actionable*—not black boxes.

**3) Optimization under real constraints**  
Formulate design and decision problems (multi-objective, robust, constrained) to identify feasible, scalable solutions.

## About Shengli (Bruce) Jiang

**Shengli (Bruce) Jiang** is an **incoming Assistant Professor** in the **Department of Chemical Engineering** at the **University of South Carolina** (Molinaroli College of Engineering and Computing), where he will establish the Materials & Product Design Group.

He earned his Ph.D. in Chemical Engineering from the University of Wisconsin–Madison (advisor: Prof. Victor M. Zavala), and his B.S. in Chemical Engineering from the University of California, San Diego (advisor: Prof. Zheng Chen). Prior to USC, he was a Postdoctoral Research Associate and Lecturer at Princeton University, working with Prof. Michael A. Webb.

Bruce’s research sits at the intersection of **computational materials science**, **data science**, and **systems engineering**. His group develops **simulation- and data-driven design frameworks** to accelerate soft materials discovery and translate it into **deployable energy, sustainability, and health technologies**.
