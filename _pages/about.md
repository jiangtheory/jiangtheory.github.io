# The Jiang Group

Welcome to **The Jiang Group** at XXX University. We are a new computational research group at the intersection of **molecular simulation**, **machine learning**, and **polymer physics**. Our goal is to accelerate the rational design of next-generation soft materials and polymers for energy storage, sustainability, and beyond.

## About Shengli (Bruce) Jiang

**Shengli (Bruce) Jiang** is an **incoming Professor** at XXX University, where he will establish The Jiang Group in the [Department of XXX].

He received his Ph.D. in Chemical Engineering from the University of Wisconsin–Madison (advisor: Prof. Victor M. Zavala), where he developed machine-learning approaches for sensor design, solvent systems, electrochemistry, and sustainability. He earned his B.S. in Chemical Engineering from the University of California, San Diego (advisor: Prof. Zheng Chen). Prior to joining XXX University, he was a Postdoctoral Associate and Lecturer at Princeton University, working with Prof. Michael A. Webb.

His research lies at the interface of computational materials science and data science. The Jiang Group develops **physics-informed machine learning** methods that couple molecular simulation with data-driven approaches to uncover interpretable structure–property relationships and rapidly design complex soft materials.
